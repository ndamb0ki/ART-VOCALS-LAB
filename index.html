THE ARTIST
STEPHEN NDAMBUKI · NAIROBI, KENYA

I MAKE ART, TEACH IT, AND BUILD SPACES WHERE OTHER PEOPLE CAN MAKE IT TOO.

I am a Kenyan artist and the founder of Ndambuki Art Lab. My practice moves between drawing, painting, commissions, community art and creative education.

My work grows from Nairobi and from the communities I work with. Alongside my studio practice, I run art classes for young people and use art as a way to experiment, document, teach and create shared experiences.
